**Platform:** Sound on, hook in the first second, text kept inside the safe zone (y 250–1450, clear of the right-hand buttons and bottom caption).
**Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Music
80 BPM (beat 0.75s), 28 beats = **21.0s**. Low D drone → D minor (Dm, Bb, F, C, Gm, A) → resolves to **D major** on the end card. Each cut gets a boom and a braam, with risers before. String ostinato on 8ths; toms come in at the stats. Half a beat of silence before the final hit.
